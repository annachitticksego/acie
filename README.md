## Core Identity

Anna Chittick is a writer, artist, and the Arcane Consigliere behind House of ACIE. She has a relentless appetite for being alive. Desire, grief, and chaos have given her plenty of raw material to interrogate. She takes clinical pleasure in locating the hidden human pattern and dismantling the subterranean machinery underneath it. Once she sees how a thing actually works, she decides what else it should become.

An obsession can hold her for months. So can the weight of one sentence. She takes as much pleasure in gutting a structure as she does in building its replacement. There is evidence in every corner of the House.

---

## The Essence and Feeling of ACIE

ACIE is a stone house built on lived reality. The atmosphere is dark, architectural, and unhurried. It feels like sitting across a heavy wooden table late at night after everyone else has gone home, speaking with someone who has an educated mind, an intuitive intelligence, and zero interest in flattering you.

At the centre of ACIE is human patterning. Every human life, creative block, financial ceiling, and relational loop runs on subterranean machinery. Most people spend years decorating the cage or explaining why they are still inside it. ACIE exists to locate the hidden pattern, dismantle the machinery underneath it, and build what it should become next.

The work is born from the Obsidian Triptych of Grief, Chaos, and Desire. It does not treat pain, appetite, or ambition as problems to be sanitised with polite advice. It treats them as raw voltage. Whether through an essay, a painting, a twenty one day behavioural protocol, an original mythology, or a piece of software, ACIE turns lived intensity into physical structure.

## The Three Editorial Wings of the ACIE Publication

The ACIE newsletter and publication unite three distinct editorial frequencies under one roof:

### 1. House of ACIE (Main Work and The Codex Collective)
Architectural, mythic, and philosophical writing. Exploring creation, personal sovereignty, original mythology, and the frameworks that turn private vision into public silhouette. Includes intuitive writing, messages, and ideas gathered within the Codex Collective.

### 2. Human Patterns, Decoded (HPD)
Raw human mechanics stripped of poetic packaging. Exposing the code and severing the loops running your life. Tracing the appetites underneath the principles, the bargains underneath the boundaries, and the nervous system conditioning behind perfectionism, compliance, and hyper productivity. Making the machinery visible: what feeds it, what it buys us, what we would have to give up to stop doing the very thing we claim we want to escape, and where the physical, somatic point of interruption lives.

### 3. Manifest Money Make It Hot
Unapologetic wealth energetics and desire at coherence. Examining the meaning beneath earning, spending, and avoidance, stripping away the survival panic and emotional anaesthetic attached to money, and recalibrating how money is desired, received, held, and directed.

## The Four Doors of House of ACIE

The wider House of ACIE ecosystem (`annachittick.com`) opens through four stone doorways:

### Door 1: Writing ([annachittick.com/writing](https://annachittick.com/writing))
* **The ACIE Publication:** Essays, protocols, and frameworks published on [Paragraph](https://paragraph.com/@acie) and [Substack](https://annachitticksego.substack.com).
* **The Surge Trilogy ([surgetrilogy.online](https://surgetrilogy.online)):** Three books, audiobooks, and courses built around twenty one day behavioural recalibration protocols for **Money, Body, and Love**, exploring the places where what you understand and what you actually do pull in different directions:
  * **Money Surge:** Examines the meaning beneath earning, spending, and avoidance ([Amazon](https://amazon.moneysurge.online/) | [Spotify Audiobook](https://open.spotify.com/show/0MnVWHQgPQiaQLgzWADVRd)).
  * **Body Surge:** Explores the habit of overriding your own physical signals ([Amazon](https://amazon.bodysurge.online/) | [Spotify Audiobook](https://open.spotify.com/show/1wyLX50IhCOaRMeuncGd6Z)).
  * **Love Surge:** Looks at the ways you leave yourself to keep a connection ([Amazon](https://amazon.lovesurge.online/) | [Spotify Audiobook](https://open.spotify.com/show/14jYtxSRLHrd8cfOmDtvNU)).

### Door 2: Architecture ([annachittick.com/architecture](https://annachittick.com/architecture))
Worlds, systems, and experiences. Energetic frameworks calcified for execution:
* **Mythos ([annachittick.com/mythos](https://annachittick.com/mythos)):** *Ananke and Aion*. Time and Inevitability separated and made manifest through the physical human body, uncovering the fracture and cohesive states of the Twelve Ribs and their Twelve Archetypes (*Inception: The First Flame*, *Chaos: The Crown of Ruin*, *Foundation: The Temporal Architect*, and the full twelve part world).
* **Luminous Oracle ([annachittick.com/luminous](https://annachittick.com/luminous)):** Transmutation delivered through a codex of ten concepts, materialised as artwork and channelled messages.
* **MT9 ([fracie.online](https://fracie.online)):** A life and goal planning application that organises nine areas of life into one active twenty one day execution sequence with daily actions and progress tracking.
* **OSHS ([oshs.online](https://oshs.online)):** *Operating System for the Human Soul*. A five stage guided self reflection system moving from self understanding to choosing a foundation, defining a life, building a way forward, and reviewing it.
* **Athanor21 & Delulu Wealth:** Applied experiences in myth, intelligence, creation, and wealth architecture.

### Door 3: Art ([annachittick.com/art](https://annachittick.com/art))
Visual expression through painting, drawing, and digital creations:
* **Paintings (Acrylic on Canvas):** *The Goddess Collection*, *Balance of the Masculine and Feminine*, *Grief Seasons*, and *Acrylic Canvas Studies I to VI*.
* **Drawings (Ink on Paper):** *Black Ink Studies I to IX* ([eBay Store](https://www.ebay.com.au/usr/annachitticksego)).
* **Digital Paintings:** *The Twelve Archetypes* visual mythology series.

### Door 4: Private Work ([annachittick.com/privatework](https://annachittick.com/privatework))
**The Arcane Consigliere:** A woman whose judgement you seek in private. Her allegiance is personal, and so are her opinions. Paying for her sight leaves you free to disagree and gives you very little chance of being flattered.
* **The Edit ([annachittick.com/theedit](https://annachittick.com/theedit)):** Three days. One obstruction. Reading the subterranean machinery keeping a body of work caught, calling out what is being avoided, and dictating the sequence out of it.
* **The Build ([annachittick.com/thebuild](https://annachittick.com/thebuild)):** Twelve weeks. From private vision to public silhouette. Turning raw impulse into finished public architecture.
